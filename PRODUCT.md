# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing React + Vite application.

## Users

The primary audiences are MI Al-Ahmad Krian Sidoarjo as the institution, its parents and students, and anyone who visits the website.

## Product Purpose

The website provides an attractive, comfortable, and seamless user experience for visitors seeking information about MI Al-Ahmad Krian Sidoarjo.

## Operating Context

Visitors access the public website to browse institutional information, school activities, teachers, achievements, articles, alumni information, and contact or registration-related content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current application is a frontend prototype and still uses mock data.
- A backend endpoint will be configured through environment variables when the backend is available.
- Complex content and workflows should use dedicated pages rather than being forced into card or modal interactions.
- Modal dialogs should remain limited to lightweight, focused interactions.
- Navigation should remain clear and comfortable for parents, students, institutional staff, and general visitors.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the existing MI Al-Ahmad logo asset.
- Preserve the existing hero background asset unless the institution provides a replacement.
- Additional institutional assets are pending and must not be fabricated.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing logo: `public/assets/logo-mi-al-ahmad.png`
- Existing hero/background building asset: `public/assets/gedung-mi-al-ahmad.jpg`
- Existing principal asset: `public/assets/principal.png`
- Institutional content currently represented by mock data in `src/data/mockData.ts`.
- Further official assets from the institution are still pending.

## Product Principles

- Make information easy to discover and understand.
- Prefer seamless page flows over interaction patterns that hide complex content in modals.
- Preserve institutional identity and use official assets as the source of truth.
- Keep the experience welcoming, comfortable, and usable across visitor types.
- Do not invent institutional facts, testimonials, or assets.
